01 — About

A multi-disciplinary maker with an engineer's brain and a storyteller's eye.

I sit at the intersection of code, motion, and language — which means I can take a project from a blank Figma file all the way to a deployed product with a launch video and bilingual documentation, without needing five different people.

I'm a Computer Science student at Wolkite University and a working full-stack developer. For the past three years I've been building web applications, editing video content, running data operations, and translating between Amharic and English for clients across Ethiopia and abroad.

I started coding because I wanted to build the tools I wished existed. I started editing because a friend asked me to fix a video and I never stopped. And I started translating because growing up bilingual meant I could bridge conversations that would otherwise stall. Today, all three disciplines feed each other: the developer in me writes clean, maintainable code; the editor in me cares about pacing and story; the translator in me makes sure nothing is lost in the handoff.

My clients tend to say the same things — that I'm fast, that I communicate clearly, and that I actually ship. I don't disappear after an invoice. I don't over-promise. I treat every project like it's going to be on my permanent portfolio, because it probably will be.
